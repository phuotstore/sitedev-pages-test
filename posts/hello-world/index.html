Welcome to your new site! This post was written on a phone with SiteDev.

Writing

Use the toolbar for bold, italic, links and images. The Front matter button at the top opens the title, tags, date and draft.

Publishing

Tap Deploy: SiteDev builds the site, shows exactly which files will be uploaded, changed or deleted, and sends only those. Offline? The deploy waits in a queue until the connection is back.

Shortcodes

A few Hugo-style shortcodes work, for example {{< youtube VIDEO_ID >}}. See Engine docs in the menu.
