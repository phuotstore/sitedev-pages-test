A weekend in the Dolomites

Nov 12, 2026 · 1 min read · Alex Rivera

We left Cortina before dawn, with headlamps and a thermos of coffee. By the time the sun hit the Tre Cime, we were already above the clouds.

The route

From Rifugio Auronzo to Locatelli, on to Pian di Cengia, then back down through Val Fiscalina. Start early: the trail is quiet until nine.
